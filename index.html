Our golden rule

Look after one another, stay in communication, and tell the coordination team your plans whenever you leave the hostel.

Entry to Kenya (eTA)

Kenyan passport holders and EAC partner-state passports (Burundi, DRC, Rwanda, South Sudan, Tanzania, Uganda) don't need an eTA.

Everyone else applies for an Electronic Travel Authorization before travel at etakenya.go.ke. Italy and Nigeria passports should be treated as needing one. Processing is normally three working days, so apply early.

Health documents

Everyone must carry a valid yellow fever vaccination certificate (yellow card).

Travellers originating from DRC or Uganda complete the Electronic Traveller Surveillance Form before disembarking. Keep the confirmation QR on your phone.

October weather

End of the long rains: quick, heavy showers, often in the afternoon. The sea is around 26–27°C and UV is very high, so wear sunscreen even when it's cloudy.

Pack

  • Reusable water bottle (mandatory)
  • Towel, swimwear, sunscreen, hat
  • Rain jacket and a dry bag
  • Mosquito repellent, long sleeves for evenings
  • Modest clothing for community visits
  • Power bank and personal medication

Leaving the hostel

A leave-out sheet is mandatory before leaving outside scheduled activities. Travel in pairs or groups, and don't walk alone after dark.

In an emergency

Get to safety, contact your coordinator with your exact location, and stay together. Never cross floodwater.

Kenya emergency: 999 or 112

Entry and health rules can change. Check the official eTA portal and your airline before you fly.

An evening in Mombasa where every country hosts its own table.
